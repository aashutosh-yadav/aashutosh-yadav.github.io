Selected Work

2025 – 2026
High-Performance URL Shortener — GitHub
Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker

Built a read-heavy backend to understand performance in practice. Got it to ~1900 req/sec at ~50ms latency under 100 concurrent users. The surprising finding: adding Redis made things slower because the database wasn't the bottleneck — worker contention was. Multi-worker scaling improved throughput ~6x. Load tested up to 500 concurrent users and mapped where the system saturates (~2500 RPS).

About

I spend most of my time thinking about backend systems — not just making them work, but understanding why they break. Caching doesn't always help. Adding workers isn't always the answer. I want to know when and why.

Recently I've been going deeper into how databases and application servers interact under concurrency — connection pooling, queueing effects, saturation points. The gap between "it works" and "it works at scale" is where I like to dig.
